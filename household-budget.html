How to use & save

The four tabs

Budget is where you plan. Compare lines your scenarios up side by side. Actuals checks the plan against a bank statement. More holds backup, restore and this guide.

Budget

The strip at the top shows what is left unallocated. It stays in view as you scroll and type. Tap it for the full breakdown against the 50/30/20 split.

Open a card to edit it. Each line is a name, an amount and how often it is paid: tap /mo to switch between monthly, yearly and weekly. Tap the three dots on a line to see its yearly cost, mark it as essential, or delete it. Deleting offers an Undo for a few seconds.

The dot beside each line shows where it counts: orange for needs, blue for wants, green for savings.

Income

Tap a name or swipe the card to switch person. Enter take-home pay and pension for each. Pension can be a flat amount or a percentage of gross salary. Tap Two people to plan on a single income.

Scenarios

Each chip under the title is a separate budget. Tap + to duplicate the current one, then change what would be different. The sliders button lets you rename, delete, or set the baseline the others are compared against.

A scenario follows the baseline: change the baseline and the same change carries into each scenario, except for anything you have altered there. The sliders button has a switch to turn this off, and Start a new year, which copies the budget with prices and pay moved up.

Year ahead

The last card on the Budget tab shows what is left in each of the next 12 months. For a line paid yearly, tap its three dots and choose the month it is paid, and it lands in that month instead of being spread. A scenario can be given a start month, and the months before it run on the baseline.

Compare

Every scenario side by side with its difference from the baseline, followed by a list of exactly which lines changed. Tap a scenario name to open it.

Actuals

Upload a CSV exported from your banking app, or try the sample data. Monzo, Starling, Barclays, Halifax, NatWest, Nationwide and HSBC are recognised, and most other banks work too. Obvious payees are categorised for you. Review queue walks you through the rest, biggest first.

Overview shows income, spending and what you kept, then spending by budget line, pot activity, recurring payments and top payees. Money moved into savings pots counts as kept, not spent. Categorise lists every payee and large transfer so you can change where it counts.

The spending chart shows the last 12 months unless you pick another time window above it: the last 3 or 6 months, one month, or any run of months. Tap i on a row to see that line month by month, whether it is rising or falling, and which payees moved. Under Paid from another account, add things this statement cannot see, such as a mortgage. They count in the chart and against your budget lines.

Similar payee names are grouped, and suggestions learn from what you have already matched. The review queue shows how much of your spending is matched. Use Some of these belong elsewhere to give single payments their own line, or mark one as a One-off to leave it out of the monthly averages. After a later upload, Review new shows only payees not seen before.

Once a statement is loaded, the Outgoings and Savings cards on the Budget tab gain a Budget / Actual switch. Anything within 3% of budget counts as on budget.

Backup and privacy

Everything is saved on this device only. Nothing is uploaded, and the file loads nothing from the internet. Open More to back up to a file, restore, copy your data, or export categorised transactions as a CSV. The bank statement is kept on this device too, so Actuals is still there next time, and Clear removes it.

Send to partner passes your budgets and matches to another phone. On that phone, open More, then Restore. Leave Merge ticked to add what is new and keep the newer version of each budget, or untick it to replace everything.

Install

When your browser supports it, More shows Install as an app, which adds the tool to your home screen. On iPhone and iPad it shows the Safari steps.

Supported files

Upload a transaction statement exported from your banking app. Everything is processed on your device — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

File types

Comma-separated (.csv) and tab-separated (.tsv) files. Most banks call this “Export as CSV”.

Banks recognised automatically

Monzo, Starling, Barclays, Halifax, NatWest, Nationwide and HSBC. Other banks usually work too — the importer looks for date, amount and description columns and adapts.

Tips

  • The last 12 months are used; older rows are filtered out.
  • You can layer several exports with Add file (e.g. two banks).
  • Monzo pot transfers feed the savings and pot views.

Install this app

Add the budget tool to your home screen so it opens like a native app and works offline.

Your browser will confirm the install.

  1. 1. Tap the Share button ↥ at the bottom of Safari
  2. 2. Scroll down and tap “Add to Home Screen”
  3. 3. Tap Add in the top corner

The app icon will appear on your home screen.

Open your browser menu and look for “Install app” or “Add to Home Screen”.

  • Chrome / Edge (Android): menu → Install app
  • Safari (iPhone/iPad): Share → Add to Home Screen
  • Desktop Chrome / Edge: install icon in the address bar
